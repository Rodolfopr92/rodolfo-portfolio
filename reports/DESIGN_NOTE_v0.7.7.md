# v0.7.7 Design Note

The three desktop cards were consolidated into one **consulting dossier**:

1. Identity and actions remain in the dossier header.
2. About and What I Deliver become two internal regions separated by one fine antique-gold rule.
3. Distribution metrics remain separate because they are evidence, not descriptive copy.
4. The standalone service CTA was removed because it repeated the diagnosis-to-implementation message and added another visual container without adding information.

The favicon follows the CASTOR-family grammar through a technical circular frame, dotted orbit, and a large central monogram. Its background is fully transparent. The R uses one oxblood body and one antique-gold outline, avoiding the prior split-color construction.
